Toolnook for Stellar · Contracts

Keep a clearer record of payments.

This command-line tool keeps a small bookkeeping ledger on your computer. It can also read a Stellar account's payment history from Horizon and bring those entries into the ledger for you to review.

What happens to a payment?

Stellar stores payment activity on the network. The ledger reads that activity from Horizon and saves bookkeeping records in a local file that you control.

Stellar account→Horizon→Local ledger→Your review

Double-entry

Each transaction has matching debit and credit amounts. This accounting rule helps keep the books balanced.

Reconciliation

Compare imported payments with your records, then review and classify them to match your bookkeeping.

Local-first

Your ledger is a file on your computer by default. You do not need to operate a database server.

Try it on your computer

You need Python 3.9 or newer. The first steps create a ledger and a couple of example account categories.

1

Install the tool

git clone https://github.com/toolnook/contracts.git
cd contracts
python -m pip install -e .
2

Start your ledger

ledgerlite init
ledgerlite account add "Stellar Wallet" asset
ledgerlite account add "XLM Revenue" revenue
ledgerlite balance

The ledger is stored at ~/.ledgerlite/ledger.json by default.

3

Optionally sync testnet

ledgerlite stellar sync \
  --account YOUR_STELLAR_PUBLIC_KEY \
  --network testnet

Use a public account address only. A secret key is never needed for this read-only sync.

Review imported entries. A payment is not automatically income or a particular business expense. Choose categories that match your records, and review them before relying on reports.

What can it help you do?

Check balances

See totals for the accounts in your ledger with ledgerlite balance.

Make reports

Create profit-and-loss, balance-sheet, and cash-flow reports from recorded entries.

Bring in other records

Import transactions from CSV or add recurring entries for regular activity.
